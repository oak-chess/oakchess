# Artes ilustradas das cartas de evento

Geradas com a ferramenta integrada de imagens. Cada cena é compartilhada pelas sete cartas do seu evento. Os nomes, raridades e bônus são renderizados pelo jogo.

Concluídos: treino, termal, usina, lago, jardim, noite, sorte, caverna, lua, forja e meditacao (77 cartas).

Pendente: vale (7 cartas). A solicitação de Dragonite foi bloqueada novamente pelo serviço de geração; o jogo mantém sua ilustração oficial anterior. A última tentativa, com os mesmos prompts, concluiu Alakazam. Os prompts abaixo registram as solicitações feitas, incluindo a que não produziu arquivo.

Os WebP finais preservam a cena completa, com 960 px de largura e qualidade 88. O jogo enquadra a cena na janela de ilustração usando `object-fit: cover`. Os PNGs oficiais antigos continuam como alternativa se o arquivo novo não carregar.

## Prompt comum

Create an original landscape painted illustration featuring this scene: {SCENE}. Recognizable Pokémon character interacting naturally with its environment, character occupying roughly half the composition, environmental depth and expressive pose. Rich watercolor and gouache textures with expressive ink outlines, atmospheric lighting and polished detail suitable for an illustration window in a collectible trading card. Landscape 3:2 composition, complete scene from edge to edge, main character's face fully visible with safe margins for a wide crop. No card frame, no text, no logos, no statistics, no interface. Original fan illustration, not a reproduction of any existing card.

### noite — prompt completo

Create an original landscape painted illustration featuring Gengar from Pokémon exploring a mysterious moonlit forest. Gengar has its recognizable round purple body, pointed ears, spiky back, red eyes and mischievous wide grin. It peeks out from behind a mossy tree, with curling purple mist, tiny fireflies, twisted branches and blue moonlight filtering through the canopy. Rich hand-painted watercolor and gouache textures, expressive ink outlines, atmospheric depth, lively composition and beautiful environmental detail suitable for the illustration window of a collectible trading card. Gengar should be clearly recognizable and occupy roughly half the image, interacting naturally with the scene. Landscape 3:2 composition. Only the finished illustration: no card frame, no text, no logos, no statistics, no interface. This is new original fan artwork, not a reproduction of any existing card.
