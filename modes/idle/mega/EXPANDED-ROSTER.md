# Expansion of the Mega catalog: 9 → 31 forms

## Approved scope

Venusaur, Charizard Y, Gyarados, Tyranitar, Sceptile, Blaziken, Aerodactyl, Scizor, Heracross, Steelix, Aggron, Absol, Manectric, Banette, Sableye, Altaria, Salamence, Gallade, Garchomp, Abomasnow, Slowbro and Medicham.

Metagross, the legendary/mythical forms and Raichu forms were not added. The original nine Mega definitions and their saved keys remain intact.

## Data and compatibility

`expansion-specs.json` declares the approved sheets, stones, habitat palettes and periodic boss mechanics. `expansion-data.js` is generated by `scripts/build-idle-mega-expansion.py`, using official PokeAPI CSV records for each form's Pokémon ID, types, Attack, Special Attack, Defense, Special Defense and Speed. Audited source values are in `padventures/expansion-stats.json`. Stat ratios are applied only to battle units; saved partner stats/genes are not rewritten.

Charizard Y uses native form **2**, distinct from the existing Charizard X form **1**. Battle setup, sprite selection, PMD cache, partner assignment, UI and model entry all respect the definition's form number. Both stones may be owned, but only one partner/form is designated at a time. Opening a Charizard's Mega panel prefers its currently designated form.

## Progression

All 22 use the existing rules: level 350, P5, perfect IV bosses; one token per paid entry; no token cost or rewards in training; three to five specific fragments plus five on the first paid win; independent five percent chance of a whole stone; forty fragments for crafting. Each specific stone requires the matching final species. Existing token/stone/fragment balances and claims are preserved by default initialization, with transactional rollback and idempotent rewards unchanged.

## Sprite handling

The original PAdventures PNGs and geometry are retained in `padventures/source/`. Native sheets are built by `scripts/build-idle-mega-padventures.py` and have manifest hashes and credits.

- Four source cardinal directions are mapped into eight renderer slots by duplication. This does not create eight original directions.
- The available source cycle is aliased to Idle/Walk/Attack and other required renderer states; no separate attack animation is claimed. Existing legal Pokémon moves and native effects remain in use.
- Charizard Y is built into `0006/0002/`, leaving all Charizard X files untouched.
- Gyarados has an approved genuine PAdventures shiny counterpart (47271), built into `0130/0001/0001/`.
- Other new shiny partners use the corresponding canonical animated Mega shiny GIF, not a recolored normal sheet. Their normal preparation form and shiny identity remain preserved.

## Bosses and presentation

Every new boss has a specific eight-second mechanic, described in the panel and declared as `bossSkill`. These are native-game adaptations rather than literal cartridge abilities. The shared dispatcher handles physical/special pulses, target groups, mana drains, slows, burns, brief stuns, capped shields and bounded healing. All damage still uses native mitigation. The original nine mechanics are unchanged.

Each of the 22 has an exclusive illustrated environment, opaque native deck and habitat-specific palette. Built-in image generation prompts and saved assets are recorded in `padventures/ART-SOURCES.json`. All final assets are under `assets/arena-<key>.webp`; no generated image outside the project is required at runtime.

The 31-form laboratory uses a search by Mega/stone and filters for owned compatible partners or obtained stones. Navigation is bounded and scrollable on smaller screens. All eighteen type names are translated. The existing confirmation before paid entry and free training flow are preserved.

## Validation

- `check-idle-mega-expanded-model.cjs`: all 22 canonical ratios/types, migration, specific crafting, form 2, identity, paid/free rewards, rollback, idempotence and all periodic mechanics.
- `check-idle-mega-expanded-browser.cjs`: all 22 paid and shiny training battles, native frames, scenes, casts, icons, crafting, search, compatibility, X/Y switching, saved genes/locks, restoration and reload.
- `check-idle-mega-catalog.cjs`: 31-form browsing, compatible-partner and obtained-stone filters, name/stone search, empty results, selected X/Y form and automatic navigation visibility.
- Existing original Mega model, integration, expansion and trio browser checks remain regression checks, now expecting a 31-form catalog.

Publication to GitHub was subsequently authorized by the user; this catalog is included in the prepared release.
